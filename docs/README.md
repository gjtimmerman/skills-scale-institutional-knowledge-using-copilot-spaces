# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation hub. This README serves as your central resource for understanding how OctoAcme runs projects and navigating our comprehensive project management documentation.

## Project Management Processes Overview

OctoAcme employs a comprehensive, lifecycle-driven project management methodology centered on five core principles: **customer-first delivery**, **iterative development**, **clear ownership**, **data-informed decision-making**, and **psychological safety**. The organization structures its projects around a cross-functional team model with defined roles and responsibilities.

**Core Team Roles:**
- **Project Managers** coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Managers** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success
- **Developers** implement features while collaborating on design and quality
- **QA/Testing teams** validate acceptance criteria and quality standards

This role-based structure ensures clear accountability and enables seamless collaboration across functional boundaries throughout the entire project lifecycle.

### The OctoAcme Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme follows a structured five-phase workflow:

1. **Initiation** — Validate business need, identify stakeholders, and define success metrics through a lightweight Project One-pager
2. **Planning** — Transform approved initiatives into actionable backlog items, establish acceptance criteria, identify dependencies, and create release plans
3. **Execution** — Emphasize daily standups, iterative development with small pull requests, continuous testing, and weekly delivery syncs
4. **Release** — Standardize deployment processes with rigorous pre-release requirements, staged deployment, and post-deploy verification
5. **Retrospective** — Capture learnings and convert insights into actionable improvements with clear owners and timelines

### Communication & Risk Management

Communication and risk management are embedded throughout OctoAcme's processes to maintain transparency and address issues proactively. The organization maintains a formal Risk Register tracking identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring of risks, with weekly reviews during project syncs. Communication follows a consistent cadence: weekly PM-PdM alignment meetings, twice-weekly team standups, and monthly stakeholder updates, supplemented by ad-hoc escalations when needed. A clear escalation path—team-level → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor—ensures issues are addressed at the appropriate level.

### Quality Assurance & Continuous Improvement

Quality assurance and continuous improvement are woven into every phase of execution. OctoAcme implements multiple testing layers—unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, and security scanning in CI—along with manual QA for feature acceptance when needed. Each project concludes with retrospectives focused on capturing what went well, identifying improvements, and converting insights into actionable items. This blameless, improvement-focused culture ensures learnings feed directly into the project backlog, fostering iterative refinement of both processes and products.

## Quick Start

**New to OctoAcme's processes?**
1. Start with the [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) for a high-level understanding
2. Review the [Roles and Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) to understand team structure
3. Dive into the specific phase documents as needed for your current project stage

**Managing an active project?**
- Keep your Project Charter updated in your project repository
- Reference the [Execution and Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) guide for day-to-day workflows
- Maintain your Risk Register using the template in [Risks and Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md)
- Schedule retrospectives at the end of each sprint or milestone using the guidance in [Retrospective and Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

Use the documentation links above or search for keywords like "risk", "communication", "deployment", "testing", or "retrospective" to find relevant sections.
