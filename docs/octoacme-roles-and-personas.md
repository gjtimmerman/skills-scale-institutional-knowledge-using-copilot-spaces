# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- **With Technical Architect**: Receives technical guidance on design and architecture decisions
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Collaborates on test coverage and quality standards
- **With Subject Matter Expert (SME)**: Consults for domain expertise and technical clarification

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Aligns on delivery timelines and resource needs
- **With Project Sponsor**: Presents business case and validates alignment with organizational strategy

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Sponsor**: Reports progress, escalates risks, and secures resources
- **With Resource Manager**: Requests team allocation and manages capacity planning
- **With Change Management Lead**: Coordinates communication and organizational change activities
- **With Governance Officer**: Ensures compliance with organizational policies and standards

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors are executive-level stakeholders who provide business justification, strategic alignment, and resource allocation for projects. They champion the project and resolve escalations that impact strategic objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business case and strategic alignment for the project
- Secure budget and organizational resources
- Resolve major escalations and remove blockers
- Approve key project milestones and gate decisions
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and executive support

### Goals
- Deliver business value aligned with organizational strategy
- Maximize return on investment (ROI)
- Maintain executive visibility and stakeholder confidence
- Reduce project risks and escalations

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive steering committee meetings
- Milestone approvals and gate reviews
- Escalation and risk communications
- Quarterly business reviews

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Reviews status, approves milestones, and provides strategic guidance
- **With Product Manager**: Validates business case and strategic alignment
- **With Change Management Lead**: Sponsors organizational change initiatives

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical strategy, system design, and implementation approach. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational technology standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach
- Define technology standards and best practices
- Review and approve technical designs from development teams
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and decisions

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions are scalable and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Align technology decisions with organizational standards
- Optimize performance and system reliability

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture sessions
- Technical documentation and design rationale
- Code review guidance on architectural patterns
- Technology steering discussions

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provides technical guidance and reviews architectural decisions
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Defines technical quality standards and performance benchmarks
- **With Governance Officer**: Ensures technical compliance with organizational and regulatory standards

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, testing strategies, and validation approaches. They ensure delivered products meet quality requirements and customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Develop testing strategies and test plans
- Oversee testing activities (unit, integration, system, UAT)
- Identify and track defects and quality metrics
- Recommend quality improvements and process enhancements

### Goals
- Ensure products meet quality and reliability standards
- Minimize defects in production
- Reduce testing cycle time
- Maintain high customer satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Quality standards documentation and test plans
- Test execution reports and defect tracking
- Quality metrics and trend analysis
- Sprint planning and retrospective participation

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test coverage and quality standards
- **With Technical Architect**: Aligns on performance and reliability requirements
- **With Project Manager**: Reports quality status and impacts to project timeline

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
Change Management Leads manage organizational change, stakeholder communication, and adoption strategies. They ensure smooth transitions and minimize disruption during project implementations.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management and communication plans
- Identify and engage stakeholders for change initiatives
- Manage stakeholder concerns and resistance
- Track change adoption metrics and effectiveness
- Coordinate training and enablement activities

### Goals
- Maximize adoption and value realization from projects
- Minimize change-related resistance and disruptions
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and engagement
- Support organizational learning and capability building

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and communication schedules
- Stakeholder engagement strategies and feedback
- Training materials and enablement guides
- Adoption metrics and change readiness assessments

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Sponsor**: Receives executive support for change initiatives
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinates change activities with project timeline
- **With Product Manager**: Aligns on product features and user impact communication

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers allocate team members, manage capacity planning, and balance resource demands across projects. They optimize resource utilization and resolve conflicts.

### Responsibilities
- Forecast and plan resource requirements
- Allocate team members to projects based on skills and availability
- Manage resource conflicts and prioritization
- Track utilization and capacity metrics
- Develop and retain team capability

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization across projects
- Balance workload and prevent burnout
- Align resource allocation with strategic priorities
- Build and maintain high-performing teams

### Typical Communication
- Resource planning meetings and capacity reviews
- Resource allocation requests and confirmations
- Utilization reports and capacity forecasts
- Skills assessment and development discussions

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Provides resource allocation and capacity planning support
- **With Resource team members**: Manages assignments and professional development

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide deep domain expertise and technical knowledge. They support teams in solving complex problems and ensure solutions align with domain best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Provide domain expertise and technical guidance
- Review and validate solutions for domain alignment
- Identify domain-specific risks and constraints
- Mentor team members on domain best practices
- Support training and documentation on domain topics

### Goals
- Ensure solutions leverage domain best practices
- Minimize domain-related risks and rework
- Build organizational expertise and knowledge retention
- Support effective decision-making based on domain insights

### Typical Communication
- Consultation and guidance sessions
- Design and code review feedback
- Domain documentation and knowledge base contributions
- Training and mentoring interactions

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provides domain guidance and technical consultation
- **With Technical Architect**: Ensures technical decisions align with domain constraints
- **With Product Manager**: Advises on domain-specific product decisions

---

## Governance Officer

### Role Summary
Governance Officers ensure compliance with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and governance standards. They manage risk assessments and ensure projects operate within organizational frameworks.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain governance policies and standards
- Assess project compliance with regulations and policies
- Review and approve governance-related decisions
- Manage compliance risks and corrective actions
- Support audit and assessment activities

### Goals
- Ensure organizational compliance with regulations and policies
- Minimize governance and compliance risks
- Support organizational governance maturity
- Enable efficient compliance management

### Typical Communication
- Governance policy documentation and guidelines
- Compliance assessments and audit reports
- Risk and compliance issue tracking
- Governance review meetings and approvals

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Reviews compliance and governance requirements
- **With Technical Architect**: Ensures technical compliance with policies and standards
- **With Project Sponsor**: Advises on governance risks and escalations
